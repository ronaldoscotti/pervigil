# Pervigil — logo generation prompts

Ready-to-send prompts for an AI image generator (Midjourney, GPT-Image / DALL·E,
Ideogram, Flux). Each is self-contained — paste one, iterate, pick a direction.

**The concept to protect across all of them:** *pervigil* is Latin for "ever-watchful;
keeping watch through the whole night." The product surfaces which AI sessions are
**blocked on you** while you're away. The emotional core is **a single lamp kept lit
through the dark** — patient, warm, awake. Not an eye (too surveillance), not a bell
(too alarm). A lamp.

**Palette (use these hex values):**
- Amber lamp glow `#F4B860` — the signature, "waiting on you / lit"
- Teal `#6FB2C4` — calm secondary, "working"
- Ground near-black `#13151B` → `#181A22`
- Ink / warm off-white `#EAE7DE`

**Form factor:** it must read at **16px** (menu-bar / favicon) and at **1024px** (app
icon). Favour one strong silhouette over fine detail.

## Take 1 — App icon: the lit lamp (primary direction)

> A minimalist macOS app icon on a dark near-black rounded-square background (#13151B to
> #181A22 subtle vertical gradient). Centered: a single small warm amber orb of light
> (#F4B860) with a soft radial bloom, like a lamp kept burning in the dark. The glow is
> calm and steady, not a flare. Faint concentric halo rings around it, very subtle.
> Premium, restrained, modern software aesthetic — think Linear, Things, Arc. Flat
> vector, crisp edges, generous negative space, no text. High contrast so it reads at
> small sizes. --v 6 --style raw --ar 1:1

## Take 2 — Symbol mark: crescent-guard around a flame

> A modern minimalist logo mark: a single upright flame or lamp-light in warm amber
> (#F4B860) cradled by a thin incomplete circular arc that curves around it like a hand
> or a night-watch shield, open at the top. Geometric, confident, built from two or
> three strokes only. Monoline, even stroke weight. Works as a solid single-color glyph.
> Dark background #181A22, amber mark, optional faint teal (#6FB2C4) accent. Vector,
> scalable, no text, lots of breathing room. --v 6 --style raw --ar 1:1

## Take 3 — Wordmark + mark lockup

> A logotype for a developer tool named "Pervigil". Set the word "PERVIGIL" in a
> refined geometric sans (Space Grotesk / Aeonik feel), letter-spacing slightly open,
> warm off-white (#EAE7DE) on a dark #13151B background. To the left, a small amber
> (#F4B860) dot-of-light with a soft glow as the brand mark, as if the "lamp" dots the
> word. Balanced, high-end SaaS branding, horizontal lockup. Crisp vector, no
> photographic texture. --v 6 --style raw --ar 3:1

## Take 4 — The night vigil (more atmospheric / marketing)

> A single small warm amber lamp glowing on a vast dark field at night, seen from
> slightly above, tiny against the darkness but unmistakably awake. Minimal, cinematic,
> a lot of empty dark space, one point of warm light (#F4B860) with a gentle bloom.
> Symbolic of keeping watch through the night. Could crop to a square app icon. Muted,
> elegant, no text, no clutter. --v 6 --ar 1:1

## Take 5 — Monospace instrument (nods to the UI's watch-face numerals)

> A minimalist app icon: a dark rounded square (#181A22) with a single amber (#F4B860)
> monospaced glyph or tick-mark centered, evoking a night watch instrument or a
> stopwatch lit in the dark. Precise, technical, restrained — like a well-made analog
> gauge. One accent of teal (#6FB2C4). Flat vector, no gradients except a faint inner
> glow, no text. Reads at 16px. --v 6 --style raw --ar 1:1

## Iteration notes for whichever you pick

- Ask for **"on a transparent background, single color amber"** to get a usable SVG-like
  glyph for the menu-bar tray icon (macOS tray wants a simple monochrome silhouette).
- Ask for a **16×16 and 32×32 legibility check** — if the mark dies small, simplify.
- Keep the amber `#F4B860` glow as the one non-negotiable brand cue; everything else can flex.
- Avoid: eyeballs, owls, bells, clock faces with hands, generic "AI" circuitry — all
  off-concept and overused.
